# Dive Planner

A simple, mobile-friendly recreational dive checklist you can fill in on the boat.

**Live site:** https://nourtalaat.github.io/dive-planner/

- Dive plan fields (site, depth, time, turn pressure, reserve, buddy…)
- Gear assembly, briefing, **BWRAF buddy check**, entry/descent, during the dive, surface & post-dive
- Optional **Nitrox** section with a MOD calculator (rounds the analysed O₂ % up, warns if the planned depth is past the MOD)
- Progress bar, collapsible sections, print / save as PDF
- Entries are saved in your browser (localStorage) — "New dive" clears them (keeps buddy, gas and reserve)

Single static `index.html`, no dependencies.

> Personal checklist — always follow your training, the dive briefing and your equipment manuals.
